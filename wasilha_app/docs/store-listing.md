## Full description

**EN**

Wasilha is the Sudanese super-app for getting around and getting things delivered.

- **Name your fare.** Set your price, and nearby drivers accept it or counter-offer. You pick the offer you like.
- **Every way to move.** Raksha (tuk-tuk), motor, or car, priced by distance.
- **Live tracking.** Watch your driver arrive on the map, chat in the app, and share your trip.
- **Delivery and food.** Send a parcel across town, or order from local cafés and restaurants.
- **Wasilha Wallet.** Top up once and pay for rides, food and tips without cash.
- **Safety first.** One-hold SOS, trusted contacts, and rated drivers.
- **Drive with Wasilha.** Go online, bid on requests, and track your earnings.
- **For stores.** Manage incoming orders and dispatch couriers.

Fully bilingual: Arabic and English.
